# The Iron Heralds
The Iron Heralds are a professional military organization that provides security, logistics, and heavy-hitting warfare services to the highest bidder. They pride themselves on discipline over glory.

## Philosophy
"Strength in Order." Unlike many mercenary bands, the Heralds have a strict code of conduct: they do not target non-combatants unless explicitly ordered by their employer.

## Hierarchy
*   **High Marshal:** The overall commander.
*   **Centurions:** Leaders of individual squads.
*   **Shield-Bearers:** The rank-and-file soldiers who form the backbone of their heavy infantry.

## Base of Operations
The Iron Citadel, a fortified keep located on the borderlands between the Whispering Woods and the Inner Reach.
