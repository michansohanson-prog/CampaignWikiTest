# Void Stalker
The Void Stalker is a terrifying predator that exists partially between planes. It can "blink" short distances, appearing as a blur of purple-black smoke before striking with razor-sharp claws.

## Traits
*   **Phase Shift:** Can become semi-incorporeal for 1 round (taking only half damage).
*   **Fear Aura:** Creatures within 10 feet must succeed on a willpower check or be "Slowed" by paralyzing fear.

## Habitat
Void Stalkers prefer damp, dark environments like caves, sewers, and the deepest parts of enchanted forests. They are solitary hunters but will work together to hunt larger prey.
