# Whispering Woods
The Whispering Woods is a massive expanse of bioluminescent flora and towering ancient oaks. It covers nearly 40% of the northern territory. The woods are famous (or infamous) for the "whispers"—sounds that travelers claim are voices from the past.

## Landmarks
*   **The Weeping Willow:** A tree so large it has its own micro-climate and ecosystem.
*   **The Hidden Grove:** A sanctuary for druids who seek to protect the forest from industrial expansion.

## Hazards
Travelers must be wary of "Root Traps" and the "Mist of Lost Memories," a magical fog that can cause travelers to wander in circles for days until they succumb to exhaustion.
