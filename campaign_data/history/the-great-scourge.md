# The Great Scourge
The Great Scourge was a period of unprecedented horror when the dead began to rise in unison across the Southern Reach. For three decades, entire cities were abandoned as necromancy-tainted rot spread through the land.

## Key Events
*   **Year 0:** The First Awakening in the city of Oakhaven.
*   **Year 15:** The Siege of the Iron Gates (The first major victory for the living).
*   **Year 30:** The Final Purge, where the source was finally sealed by the Order of Scribes.

## Cultural Impact
To this day, many southern regions remain wary of large cemeteries and maintain strict "Purification Rites" during full moons. Many families still carry "Scourge Charms" as protection.
