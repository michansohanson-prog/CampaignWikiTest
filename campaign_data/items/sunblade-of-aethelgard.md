# Sunblade of Aethelgard
A legendary blade forged in the heart of a dying star (or so the myths say). The blade pulses with a warm, golden light that intensifies when it is drawn against creatures of unlife.

## Properties
*   **Light:** Sheds bright light in a 30-foot radius.
*   **Radiant Strike:** Deals an extra 1d8 radiant damage to undead targets.
*   **Warmth:** The wielder feels no effect from extreme cold environments.

## History
Originally used by the Paladins of Aethelgard to defend against the Great Scourge, only one blade is known to still exist in the current age. It is said to be hidden somewhere in the Whispering Woods.
